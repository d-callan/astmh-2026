What Is BRC Analytics?

The premise

Tools and workflows that let researchers of any background, technical depth, or budget answer the questions that matter to them — and discover questions they never knew they could ask.

What that means in practice
  • Free, browser-based, open — curated pathogen data and analysis on BRC Analytics, running on Galaxy
  • No install, no cluster, no code required to start — but nothing is hidden from those who want to look
The non-negotiables
  • Every run recorded — shareable, inspectable histories, not screenshots
  • Same tools for everyone — what we use internally is what you get
Who we are
The identity slide. Stress the audience spread on purpose: bench parasitologist with no cluster, computational person with no wet-lab context, student with a laptop — same tools, same data, same price (free). "Same tools for everyone" is the honest claim: what's built is built for public use — but be upfront that much of today is forward-looking; several workflows are still in development, and that's the point of showing them.
